Physics model and assumptions (reveal after investigating)

The cart is released from rest and the track is frictionless. The release point is a distance d = 0.35 m up the inclined line (from the point where it would meet the horizontal). The short, smoothly joined curve replaces the final section of that incline, so it adds no extra vertical drop. Thus Δh = (0.35 m) sin θ and conservation of energy gives v = √(2g · 0.35 sin θ), using g = 9.81 m/s². The velocity is unchanged along the horizontal section. Each displayed reading is this ideal speed multiplied by an independently randomized factor between 0.95 and 1.05.

Prediction: Plotting v² against sin θ should produce a straight line through the origin, with gradient 2gd = 6.87 m²/s², approximately.
