Working model used in 05a

Build the energy account from the system boundary

For a single object, the work–energy theorem is ΔK = ΣWi. For the worksheet's simplified mechanical-energy model, use the ledger below. Do not count the same interaction twice.

ΔK = ΣWi
ΔUg = mgΔy
Us = ½k(Δx)²
ΔEmech = ΔK + ΔUg + ΔUs

Worksheet convention: g = 10 m s-2. Friction is generally treated as external in the mechanical-energy ledger; the finer-detail questions separately track internal energy generated by relative sliding.

Worksheet pp. 1-2 · What is Wext?

Wext is the net work done by forces external to the selected system. For a single object, the AP work-energy theorem is ΔK = ΣWi.

Before writing an equation, identify the physical objects inside the system. Everything else is the surroundings.

Gravity and springs · no double-dipping

Earth inside: represent the gravitational interaction with ΔUg; do not also include gravitational work in Wext.

Ideal spring inside: represent the spring interaction with ΔUs; do not also include spring work in Wext.

Mechanical energy and force type

Emech = K + U. Here U may include gravitational and elastic potential energy; internal energy is not part of mechanical energy.

Gravity and ideal-spring forces are conservative. Sliding friction and drag are nonconservative.

Friction · work vs internal energy

This worksheet usually treats friction as external in the mechanical-energy ledger. Work by friction on one object depends on that object's displacement.

For sliding contact, internal energy generation depends on the relative sliding distance: ΔUint = fkdrel ≥ 0.
