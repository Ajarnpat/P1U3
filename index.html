L1

Energy, systems, and energy stores

  • Energy, systems, and energy stores
    Identify the energy present in a chosen system, distinguish kinetic from potential energy, and describe mechanical energy as K + U.
  • Translational kinetic energy
    Use K = ½mv², reason proportionally, and recognise the reference-frame dependence of measured kinetic energy.
  • Potential energy: gravity and springs
    Treat potential energy as a system quantity and use gravitational and spring potential-energy relationships appropriately.

The enquiry HTML includes matching, reveal notes, hints and worked solutions; its interactive elements are built into the notes.
